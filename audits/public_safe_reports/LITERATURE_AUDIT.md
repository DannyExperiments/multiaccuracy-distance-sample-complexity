# Q572 literature and target check

**27 November 2026. High confidence in source fidelity and dependency attribution. Moderate confidence on priority after a bounded search.** The result gives the optimal leading group-count exponent for fixed small additive error and a sample-polynomial empirical LP. No earlier result giving these general statistical bounds for distance to exact multiaccuracy was found. The finite-parameter bounds leave polynomial and logarithmic factors.

## The exact source

- Derhake, Devic, Hansen, Liu and Sharan, *Auditability and the Landscape of Distance to Multicalibration*, ITCS 2026, §1.2.6, article pp. 48:8–9, explicitly leaves open statistically and computationally feasible auditing of distance to multiaccuracy. The full version's §§4.1 and 4.3 give Definitions 28–29 and Proposition 36, respectively. The target is marginally weighted L1 distance to predictors with zero mean residual on every specified group. Proposition 36 already gives the perfect-information linear program. Proposition 35's Fibonacci example shows sensitivity of distance relative to worst-group bias. It does not exhibit two statistically close labeled laws having separated distances or supply a sample rate. The catalog explicitly asks about labeled IID samples, finite full-support domains, known f, and group membership queries. [ITCS proceedings](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.48) · [Full version, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16930#S4) · [Pinned catalog entry](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/computer_science_ai/classical-statistical-machine-learning.md#q572)

## Matrix dependencies

- Alon and Vũ, *Anti-Hadamard Matrices, Coin Weighing, Threshold Gates, and Indecomposable Hypergraphs*, JCTA 79(1), 133–160 (1997). Their §2 Main Theorem measures the largest **absolute** inverse entry, with binary 0/1 matrices in the class indexed by 1. The displayed logarithm is base 2. Combining the stated upper and lower bounds gives log₂ χ(k) = (1/2)k log₂ k − Θ(k) as k tends to infinity. The arbitrary-order construction in §2.4 combines the power-of-two blocks using supermultiplicativity. Applying only the power-of-two subsection or padding from the previous power of two would lose the leading coefficient at intermediate dimensions. Each finite k has an extremal invertible binary matrix by finiteness and the identity example. [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/jcta.1997.2780) · [Author PDF §2](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nalon/PDFS/av1.pdf#page=4) · [Arbitrary-order argument](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nalon/PDFS/av1.pdf#page=9)
- The same authors' Proposition 3.4.3 already appends the first coordinate vector to an ill-conditioned binary matrix to create a rank-k binary system with a large ratio of kernel coordinates. The proof here adapts that construction, with an arbitrary extremal column and its coordinate vector. Magnitudes determine the marginal and signed coefficients determine the predictor offsets. This reuse covers the specific rectangular design as well as the asymptotic inverse bound. The rare-label two-law experiment and the feasible-predictor clipping argument are the statistical steps. [Author PDF §3.4](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nalon/PDFS/av1.pdf#page=17)

## Nearby results and the restricted companion

- Okoroafor, *The Sample Complexity of Multicalibration with an ERM Oracle* (2026), §5, estimates residual-test metrics. Its §5.4 random-sign testing argument yields an analogous square-root dimension lower bound using constant predictors, for ordinary multicalibration error. Lu, Noarov and Roth, *Calibrated Multiaccuracy: Deterministic and Randomized Sample Complexity* (2026), studies learning approximate predictors with calibration and residual-test criteria. Both address tasks different from weighted distance to the entire exact multiaccuracy constraint set. [Okoroafor §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14363v1#S5) · [Lu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03305)
- The independent-atom formula in the companion reduces to discrete L1 functional estimation. Given the atom and residual u = Y − f(X), generate a sign whose conditional mean is u. Use score zero on the uncovered atom. If R is the resulting distribution on atom/sign pairs and R' flips the sign, the exact atom formula gives dMA = ||R − R'||₁/2. Disjoint sample batches give independent draws from the two distributions. Conversely, taking equally likely draws from P and known uniform U and labeling by the source, with singleton groups and f = 1/2, gives dMA = ||P − U||₁/4. Jiao, Han and Weissman's known-reference and unknown-reference results apply in their stated sample regimes. The appendix presents the direct streaming counter estimator and its elementary lower bound without claiming restricted minimax optimality. [Jiao–Han–Weissman, Theorems 3 and 6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.00807)
